Findings: tiny looped reasoners are strongly contracting (lambda_1 < 0). They solve in a few loops, and with several
valid answers each map converges to ONE solution regardless of the starting latent (the model learns a deterministic
choice, so there are no competing basins). No transient chaos, no fractal settle-time structure.
Reading: EqR's transient chaos is not a generic property of looped reasoners at any size. It appears to need scale or
per-loop depth (EqR runs many transformer passes per loop). This bears on Lai et al.'s expectation that most
reasoning models show fractal basins.
