# S4: survival-regression spectroscopy on toys with fluctuating stretching (numpy, 2026-10-28): FAILED
Script `s4_survival_regression.py` (expectations + Amendment 1 in the docstring), raw `s4_survival_regression.json`.

| toy | truth (escape direction) | run 1 (future lifetime ~ past stretches) | run 2 (window survival ~ window stretches, logistic) |
|---|---|---|---|
| ROUGH-A (a depends on x2) | k = 3 | k* = 2; decision coefficient POSITIVE (t +12) | k* = 2 (z -96); k = 3 z -36 |
| ROUGH-B (a depends on x1, confounded) | k = 3 | k* = 1 (t -21) | k* = 1 (z -131) |
| FILAM (hole in x1) | k = 1 | no signal (\|t\| < 1) | k* = 3; S_1 coefficient strongly POSITIVE (z +142) |

Why it fails: survival-stretching correlations are dominated by GEOMETRIC CONFOUNDS, not by the thermodynamic weight
exp(-S_esc). Examples: in FILAM the hole sits where x1 stretches least (1.8), so the escapers look less stretched; in
ROUGH escapers have large y, which inflates the off-diagonal Jacobian terms. The exp(-S_esc) principle holds for weights
over orbits of an invariant set (periodic-orbit / large-deviation level), not as a regression across positions in a map
whose stretching varies with position.
Lesson: escape statistics without geometry cannot identify the escape direction. Together with S0-S3, every
precision-free observable tried so far is either degenerate (B&L) or confounded.
